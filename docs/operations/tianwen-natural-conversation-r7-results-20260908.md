## Confirmed defect and separate limits

The evidence projection preserves every byte, including pure-whitespace answer units. The domain parser required every unit to have a claim and required every quotation to contain nonblank text. No truthful representation could satisfy both rules for a blank line. Read-only replay of actual native captures reproduced U1 empty-claims rejection, U4/U7 whitespace-quotation rejection, and the first study's empty-claims rejection. U8 omitted whitespace units; U6 also omitted some substantive units and is not explained entirely by blank lines.

The prospective fix must retain lossless projection and all source/model/native proof binding. Only an exactly bound, entirely whitespace unit may carry zero claims or a narrowly valid non-factual whitespace claim. Substantive units still require complete coverage and exact nonblank quotations. R7 remains terminal; any fixed version receives a separate frozen cohort. The formatting fix alone does not establish that the model's semantic judgments or response content are accurate.
