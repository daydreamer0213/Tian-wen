For studies inspect original/prospective purposes, independently attributed feedback, pre-proposal material identity, actual generated trial answers, both checks per arm, unchanged consensus, candidate activation or inactivity and later task behavior separately. The finite pilot does not establish superiority, and this finite browser cohort does not establish long-term benefit. A naturally untriggered branch is not a passed branch. No activation means no observed adoption; lack of a successful improvement branch must remain explicit.

Engineering acceptance requires exact frozen bytes/proofs, actual non-scripted use, no lost/stuck ordinary task caused by the new integration, valid typed receipts for completed review, and no invalid audit becoming active through live/recovery paths.
