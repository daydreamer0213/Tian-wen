## Evidence and outcome rules

For every task, check admission occurs before the answer, current quality v2, original restrictions preserved, two distinct native review Sessions on identical material, retained raw outputs and exact captured-value proof, and host consensus. Independently inspect the complete answer against the original request; a two-model met is not itself proof of semantic correctness. Positive controls must not all be rejected merely because a checker is skeptical.

V2/V4 may support a study but never rewrite V1/V3's original verdict. A study needs actual compatible counterevidence. Freeze generated cases before proposal, keep trials blind to criteria and prior answers, keep both reviewers blind to version and each other, retain every arm and terminal outcome. Only the unchanged acceptance rule may activate guidance. Record actual V6 behavior; do not claim activation or rollback if not observed.

Separate diagnostic controls may supply fixed good/bad answers to the real review helper to test the evaluator; they must be frozen in a separate record before calls, labeled as diagnostics, and never entered as natural-user tasks, study support or browser acceptance. They cannot overwrite or regrade U/V tasks.
