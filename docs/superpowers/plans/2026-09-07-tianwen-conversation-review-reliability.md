## Design and boundaries

R4 retained two demonstrated failures: extraction weakened an explicit output-only constraint; review accepted source-dependent assertions stronger than the original evidence. The original text was present, so this is not a storage or transport defect. More lenient activation thresholds would not repair it.

The smallest change reuses native read-only one-shot judgments and the existing ledger. Admission must preserve explicit output, exclusion and uncertainty constraints. Original direct-user instructions remain authoritative even when the model's criteria omit or weaken them. Quoted content and prior assistant claims do not become instructions or verified facts.

Current quality contract becomes v2; exact v1 remains readable and digest-stable. Each v2 task review and each blind trial review uses two distinct native Sessions on the same frozen material. The first reconstructs requirements and checks the whole result; the second independently searches for unsupported source-dependent claims, certainty upgrades and instruction violations, then checks the whole result. Neither receives the other's answer or the method version. Both keep their full result and persisted proof. Host-derived verdict: identical met/not-met results are conclusive; any disagreement or inconclusive result is inconclusive. Unavailable or malformed checks cannot establish success. External effects and subjective satisfaction retain their existing inconclusive boundary.

Old task reviews and studies are never rewritten, regraded or credited as v2. Existing future-only quality migration retires incompatible active guidance. New v2 reviews/arms cannot bypass the two-check receipt. Accepted-study recovery verifies both review Sessions. Executions, proposals and reviews remain independent. No new service, dependency, tool permissions, external skill, slash command or user formatting requirement.
