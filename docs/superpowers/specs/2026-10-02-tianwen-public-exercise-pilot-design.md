# Independent public exercise pilot design (2026-10-02)

## Why this is different
The formal DEV has no qualified repeated problems. Re-auditing it cannot create evidence, and another project's active files cannot serve as our backlog. The owner explicitly authorized designing a question/environment. This finite pilot uses genuinely unknown native answers against independently published Exercism requirements and canonical tests. It is **controlled public-exercise evidence**, never natural backlog, owner feedback, statistical improvement, or full learning/release proof. Public tasks may be present in model training; no novelty or contamination-free claim is made.

## Alternatives and decision
Another maintenance report repeats the recent unsuccessful sourcing strategy. A synthetic failed source can test mechanics but cannot establish independent outcomes. Use a single bounded group of three different public exercises instead, with original upstream expected values preserved. Do not choose exercises after observing results, require failures, deliberately damage candidates, or repeat a task for a better answer.

## Stop and next entry

One problem or missing/invalid material also cannot constitute repeated-source qualification. Two genuinely checked compatible problems plus an original valid successful counterexample permit at most one automatic research after the existing selector and five-case independent checker contract are actually satisfied. Independent truth/cases are frozen before the first ordinary answer; actual original request/context/criteria/file materials are bound before research proposal, without using candidate outputs to choose truth. Reserved bob and matching-brackets upstream materials are frozen but never run as ordinary sources to force eligibility. The thin supplier delegates to the existing closed five-case factory, with bob/matching-brackets as the two fixed reserves; no default or unsupported-task fallback.
