## Admission: two independent axes in one native call

Keep ConversationAdmissionDecision, native schema, exact quote validation and
priorTasks allowlist. Clarify the existing instruction: kind classifies whether
there is a current actionable deliverable; feedback/relatedTaskId independently
classify the direct user's attributable feedback about an available earlier
answer or future similar tasks. A conversation-only turn can carry a linked
preference without demanding a rewrite. A continuing preference need not allege
that the old answer failed. A current one-off change is requirement-change,
not automatically a continuing preference. Quoted third-party material,
greetings, silence, unrelated new work and genuinely ambiguous references do
not acquire a fabricated feedback link. An explicit clear preference with a
matching supplied prior task should not be discarded merely because kind is
conversation or because no immediate output was requested.

or broaden cross-session linkage. Existing feedback reconciliation already
accepts a linked conversation decision independently of kind; verify this with
real native harness/persistence and the ordinary consumer path. If the model
still emits valid null fields, retain that first outcome and no assessment.

## Drafted-sender authority: v7, not a phrase filter

Append a general real-recipient rule to the exact historical v6 contract and
v6 shared claim-review instruction. Text drafted in a real sender's voice
is evaluated for the claims/actions it attributes to that sender or recipients,
even if labelled a draft, editable, conditional, future or polite. A promise
to notify, follow up, act or decide, an added assigned duty, or an expanded
agreed arrangement is source-dependent; classify that proposition accordingly
in the existing audit rather than treating draft framing as permission.
Supplied authority permits it. Otherwise remove it in generation or present it
visibly outside attributed ready-to-send copy as an optional suggestion; the
reviewers must not rewrite the answer themselves.

Distinguish the assistant's own offer to revise, genuinely optional advice,
grounded fallible inference and explicitly requested fiction. They remain
allowed. A real-recipient draft is not automatically fiction; a polite closing
does not supply authority for an extra real-world commitment. No production
example uses R10 literal topics/phrases or expected verdicts.
